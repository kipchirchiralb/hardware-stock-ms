- PUBLIC
- a website listing all the products in stack 
- provide contact details  - link to whatsapp

- ADMIN
- manage products (add, edit, delete)
- each product - name, price, description, image, measurement unit, quantity, instock count
- admin login to manage products


- SALES REPRESENTATIVE
- view products
- Sell products to customers

- DATABASE DESIGN 
- Tables:
  - Users: id, name, email, password, role (admin/sales representative)
  - Products: id, name, price, description, image_url, measurement_unit, quantity, instock_count
  - Sales: id, sales_rep_id, customer_name, customer_contact, sale_date
  - Sales_Items: id, sale_id, product_id, quantity_sold, total_price
